歡迎來到本站。

這是一個以「互動式世界地圖 × 國家深度內容」為核心的探索型網站。網站最大的特色，是結合互動地圖、國家資訊整理與 AI 輔助內容生成，希望讓使用者能更直覺地理解世界各地的地理、文化、政治與國際關係。

本站由一位高中生獨立建立與維護。

建立這個網站的起點其實很單純：
「如果完全不懂程式，只靠 AI，能做出怎樣的網站？」

因此，本站本身也是一個持續進行中的 AI 實驗與學習計畫。從地圖介面、國家資料整理、雙語內容，到互動功能與頁面設計，許多部分皆透過 AI 協助完成，再由站長進行整合、調整與驗證。

網站內容

  • 🗺️互動式世界地圖
  • ⛰️地形、人口、板塊、地震等圖層資訊
  • 📊國家基本資料與統計資訊
  • 📚各國深度介紹與背景脈絡
  • 🌐國際關係與台灣相關內容
  • 🌏雙語（繁體中文／英文）閱讀體驗

本站希望做到的不只是「查資料」，而是讓使用者能從地圖、空間與結構化內容中，更完整地理解一個國家與它在世界中的位置。

AI 生成內容聲明

本站有相當比例的內容由 AI 協助生成，因此資訊可能存在錯誤、過時或不完整的情況。我們會盡可能進行人工檢查與修正，但仍建議使用者自行交叉查證重要資訊。

引用與內容使用

歡迎引用本站內容。只要標示來源並附上本站連結，即可進行非商業引用與分享。

若您發現本站引用資料有署名不完整、來源標示錯誤，或其他內容問題，也非常歡迎來信告知，我們會盡快修正。

Welcome to this site.

This is an exploration-focused website built around “Interactive World Map × In-depth Country Content”. The core idea is to combine an interactive map, curated country information, and AI-assisted content generation, so users can understand geography, culture, politics, and international relations more intuitively.

This site is independently created and maintained by a high school student.

The starting point was simple:
“If you don’t know how to code at all, what kind of website can you build using only AI?”

In that sense, the site itself is an ongoing AI experiment and learning project. From the map interface and data organization to bilingual content, interactive features, and page design, many parts are drafted with AI support, then integrated, adjusted, and verified by the site owner.

What’s Included

  • 🗺️Interactive world map
  • ⛰️Layers: terrain, population, tectonic plates, earthquakes, and more
  • 📊Country basics and statistical summaries
  • 📚In-depth country introductions and context
  • 🌐International relations and Taiwan-related topics
  • 🌏Bilingual reading experience (Traditional Chinese / English)

The goal is not only to “look things up”, but to help users understand a country and its place in the world through maps, spatial context, and structured content.

AI-Generated Content Notice

A substantial portion of the content is generated with AI assistance, so some information may be incorrect, outdated, or incomplete. We do our best to review and correct it, but we still encourage users to cross-check important details.

Citation & Reuse

You’re welcome to cite and share content from this site for non-commercial use, as long as you credit the source and include a link to the site.

If you notice missing attribution, incorrect source labeling, or other content issues, please email us and we’ll fix it as soon as possible.
